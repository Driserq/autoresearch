# Mental-state app — product research

Research memo on two adjacent app concepts raised in an August 2026 brief:

- **A — the interrupt.** Catch a dysregulated state in the moment (stress spike →
  withdrawal → dopamine seeking) and prompt the physical action that breaks it.
- **B — the energy planner.** Plan the day around capacity rather than clock time.

**Recommendation: build A, with an n-of-1 experiment loop layered on top.** B is a
feature of A later, not a wedge — the market has a funded incumbent (Lifestack, YC),
requires a wearable, and rests on chronotype/ultradian claims that are weaker than
the productivity press suggests.
